## Vowels (5 qualities × length)

| | Front | Central | Back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Close | i (iː) | | u (uː) |
| Mid | e (eː) | | o (oː) |
| Open | | a (aː) | |

The /i e a o u/ system is the most common vowel inventory in PHOIBLE — a deliberately
classic, vowel-forward base.

## Length & moras (the "moraic" pillar)

Length is **phonemic on both tiers**:

- **Vowel length:** short vs. long — `a` vs. `aː` (e.g. *pa* vs. *paː*).
- **Geminate consonants:** singleton vs. long — `t` vs. `tː` (e.g. *ata* vs. *atːa*).

Both add a mora, so syllable weight is real and audible. Geminates apply to obstruents,
nasals, and liquids; glides /w j/ and /h/ do not geminate (provisional — confirm in
Lesson 06).

## Romanization (provisional)

- Long vowels → macron: **ā ē ī ō ū**.
- Geminates → doubled letters: **pp tt kk bb dd gg mm nn ll rr ss**.
- /ŋ/ → **ng**, /j/ → **y**; all other letters as in the IPA above.
- Example: /paːlːu/ → *pāllu*.
