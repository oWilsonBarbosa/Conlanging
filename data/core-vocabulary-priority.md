# Core vocabulary priority — least-borrowed, most-ancient concepts

Source: **Tadmor (2009), the Leipzig-Jakarta list** (via Concepticon `Tadmor-2009-100`).
Across 1,460 meanings in 41 languages, these 100 are the most resistant to borrowing
and the most stable/reconstructible over time — i.e. the safest concepts to coin as
native, inherited roots in the language rather than treat as loanwords.

- **BORROWED_SCORE** — higher = less often borrowed across languages.
- **AGE_SCORE** — higher = more ancient / reconstructible to deep proto-stages.
- **COMPOSITE_SCORE** — overall stability (the ranking below).

> **fire is rank #1** — the single most stable concept worldwide, and also #1 by age
