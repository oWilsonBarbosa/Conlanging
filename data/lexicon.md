# Lexicon

Inventory: `p t k b d ɡ m n s h l r j w` / `i a u` (`y`=/j/, `g`=/ɡ/).
Forms shown as *absolute / thematic* where a root is established; otherwise a single
citation form. All entries here gloss "fire" or "nose".

## FIRE

### Established roots
| Root | Forms | Meaning |
|------|-------|---------|
| TAR- | atar / adra | sacred & divine fire — the consuming, holy flame |
| NAR- | nār / nara | fire-as-substance — the luminous flame |
| UK-  | uku / ukwa | domestic & nurturing fire — the hearth flame |
